Watershed Image Segmentation

The Watershed Algorithm is a region-based image segmentation technique inspired by topographic interpretation of grayscale images. It views images as 3D terrains: the intensity value of each pixel acts like elevation.

Real-World Analogy

Imagine pouring water over a terrain with hills and valleys. The water flows into the nearest catchment basins. Boundaries are formed where water from different basins would meet — these boundaries become the segmentation lines.
